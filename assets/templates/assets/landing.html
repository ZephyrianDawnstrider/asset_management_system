Workflow

From new equipment to a clear custody trail

These steps describe the live product workflow. No employee or inventory records are shown publicly.

01

Register

Add an asset with its category and unique identifier. New assets enter the register as available.

02

Assign

Open an employee record and assign an available asset. Current custody updates with a history event.

03

Return

Return the asset before assigning it to someone else. Both transitions remain visible in the timeline.

04

Review

Use the operations overview, search the register, inspect custody history, or export matching assets.

Operations

Start with what needs a look

The staff overview brings available assets and recent custody events above the employee coverage table. Each item links to its record or timeline.

Guardrails

History stays with the asset

Assignment and return events remain available after an asset is retired or an employee is deactivated. Register exports apply the current search and filters across all pages.

Fieldnote Assets is a portfolio demonstration. Access to actual records is limited to authorized staff.
